JWT authentication middleware
Once you've rotated the secret, we'll create:

backend/src/middleware/auth.middleware.ts

It will:
- Read the JWT from the HTTP-only cookie.
- Verify the JWT signature and expiry.
- Extract userId, businessId, roleId and roleName.
- Attach the authenticated identity to the Express request.
- Reject unauthenticated requests with HTTP 401.
