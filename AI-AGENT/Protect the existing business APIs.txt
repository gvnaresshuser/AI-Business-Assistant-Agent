Protect the existing business APIs

We have secured the AI Agent conversation routes. However, your existing business and analytics APIs are not yet protected by authentication.

Next, we'll review app.ts and organize the routes so that:
- Public routes: health checks and login.
- Protected routes: businesses, products, customers, orders, inventory, expenses, purchases and analytics.
- Role-based restrictions: Admin, Manager and Staff.
We'll also ensure the authenticated businessId is used rather than trusting a business ID supplied by the frontend.

Our intended structure:
Public routes
- /api/health
- /api/database/health
- /api/auth/login

Authentication middleware
Protected routes
- Businesses and categories
- Products and customers
- Sales and purchases
- Inventory and expenses
- Payments and refunds
- All analytics APIs
- AI Agent APIs
