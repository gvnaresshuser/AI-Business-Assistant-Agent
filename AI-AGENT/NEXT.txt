My recommendation for the next phase: Develop a Business Intelligence AI Assistant that uses your existing analytics APIs to answer questions in natural language.

For example:
- Which product category generated the highest revenue?
- Which vendor received the highest purchase value?
- What is my current inventory valuation?
- Compare sales revenue with refunds.
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
This would be a meaningful extension of your existing Gemini-powered AI Assistant, rather than building another disconnected module.
