Cookie verification
You mentioned that Postman displayed access_token. That confirms the cookie exists, but the copied token itself does not establish whether HttpOnly is enabled.

In Postman, inspect the cookie properties and verify:
- HttpOnly: true
- Secure: false for your current localhost HTTP development setup
- SameSite: Lax
