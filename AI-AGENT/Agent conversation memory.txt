Agent conversation memory

The next logical step is to make the agent remember the conversation within a session.

For example:

User: What are my total sales?

Agent: Your total sales are ₹30.23 crore.

User: Which month had the highest sales?

Agent: The highest-sales month was December 2025.

The agent should understand that the second question continues the same business discussion.

We'll implement this using your existing agent_sessions_biz_agent and agent_messages_biz_agent tables, without disturbing the working tools.
